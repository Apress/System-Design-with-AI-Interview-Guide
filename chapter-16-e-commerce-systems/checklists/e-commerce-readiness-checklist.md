# E-Commerce Readiness Checklist

Before scaling commerce commitments, confirm:

- Cart and reservation are separate
- Hold and commit tokens exist
- Inventory/payment/fulfillment freshness budgets exist
- Checkout idempotency is tied to intent
- Confirmed requires token chain
- Modification cutoff rules exist
- Refund decisions are replayable
- Stop authority exists
- Replay coverage hard stop exists
- ROCS metrics trigger posture changes
