# ROCS: E-Commerce

| Dimension | Pressure | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Reliability | Commitments hold under load. | Idempotency, tokens, cutoffs. |
| Observability | Decisions are replayable. | Replay cards, correlation IDs. |
| Cost | Retry/rework/compensation. | Budgets, caps, escalation. |
| Sustainability | Trust does not drift. | Stop authority, refusal lines. |
