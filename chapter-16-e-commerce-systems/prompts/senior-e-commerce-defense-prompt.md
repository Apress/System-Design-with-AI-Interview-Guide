# Senior E-Commerce Defense Prompt

Challenge my design as an interviewer.

Ask why e-commerce is not just catalog, cart, checkout, payment, inventory, fulfillment, and notifications.

Evaluate whether I can defend:

- Commitment engine framing
- Hold vs commit
- Freshness as admissibility
- Token chain before Confirmed
- Refusal lines
- Bounded churn
- Stop authority
- ROCS
- Replay evidence

Then give me a stronger senior-architect answer.
