# Average Load Is a Lie Checklist

Do not stop at average QPS.

Ask:

- What is peak QPS?
- What is the peak-to-average ratio?
- How long does the burst last?
- Which products or entities become hot?
- What is the read/write mix?
- What downstream systems receive fan-out?
- What retries automatically?
- Which queues grow first?
- Which paths are commitment-critical?
- How does AI multiply the work?
