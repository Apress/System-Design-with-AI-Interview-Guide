# Scale Readiness Checklist

Before scaling a system, confirm:

- The customer promise is clear
- Peak load is estimated
- Burst duration is known
- Read/write mix is known
- Hot keys are identified
- Fan-out is mapped
- Queues are visible
- Retry behavior is bounded
- Commitment points are protected
- Cache trust labels exist
- Shard keys follow access patterns
- AI inference budgets exist
- Safe degraded modes exist
- Replay evidence exists
