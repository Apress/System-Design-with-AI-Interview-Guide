# Senior Scale Defense Prompt

Challenge my scale design as an interviewer.

Ask why I chose my scaling patterns instead of simply adding Redis, Kafka, autoscaling, and sharding.

Evaluate whether I can defend:

- What promise is under pressure
- What can be approximate
- What must be exact
- Where work queues
- What retries are allowed
- How idempotency is enforced
- Where caching helps or hurts
- Why the shard key fits the access pattern
- How AI fan-out changes the scale model
- What safe degraded mode exists
- What evidence proves the system remained trustworthy
