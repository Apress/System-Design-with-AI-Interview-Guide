# Capacity Estimation Review Prompt

Review my capacity estimation.

Check whether I considered:

- Peak traffic, not only average traffic
- Burst duration
- Read/write ratio
- Payload size
- Storage growth
- Bandwidth
- Downstream fan-out
- Queue risk
- Retry behavior
- Commitment points
- AI inference work
- Evidence and replay

Then improve my estimate and identify the bottleneck most likely to break trust.
