# Self-Evaluation Prompt

Copy and paste this prompt after you write a system design answer.

```text
Review my system design answer using the seven-step method.

Evaluate whether I clearly covered:
1. Requirements and constraints
2. Domain and users
3. Customer journey
4. Business capabilities and boundaries
5. High-level architecture
6. Critical component deep dive
7. Validation, scale, and stress testing

Also evaluate:
- AI authority boundaries
- Evidence and replay
- Degradation behavior
- Ownership
- Cost and latency trade-offs

Give me a score out of 10 and rewrite my answer as a stronger senior-level response.
```
