# First Five Minutes Template

Use this template when an interviewer gives you a broad system design prompt.

## Scope

Are we designing the full platform or one critical flow?

## Users

Who are the primary actors?

## Core Use Cases

What are the must-have flows?

## Scale

What traffic, data volume, geography, and peak behavior should I assume?

## Quality Attributes

What matters most: latency, availability, consistency, cost, security, compliance, trust, or operational simplicity?

## Out of Scope

What should I intentionally not design unless time allows?

## Method Statement

I will clarify requirements, model the domain, follow the customer journey, define capabilities, propose the high-level architecture, deep dive into the riskiest component, and then stress-test the design.
