# Senior Messaging Defense Prompt

Challenge my messaging design as an interviewer.

Ask why messaging is not just Kafka topics, partitions, consumer groups, retries, and DLQ.

Evaluate whether I can defend:

- Side-effect commitment engine framing
- Side-effect boundary
- eventId/businessTxnId/effectId
- Durable idempotency
- Streams vs queues
- Ordering scope
- Retry budgets
- DLQ simulate-first replay
- Stop authority
- Replay evidence

Then give me a stronger senior-architect answer.
