# Senior Ride-Sharing Defense Prompt

Challenge my ride-sharing design as an interviewer.

Ask why ride-sharing is more than geohash, Kafka, Redis, and a matching service.

Evaluate whether I can defend:

- Marketplace commitment engine framing
- Hold versus commit
- Freshness budgets
- Bounded churn
- Pricing receipts
- Fairness control loop
- Inference DAG budgets
- Phase-aware conflict handling
- Safety/fraud hard stops
- Replay evidence

Then give me a stronger senior-architect answer.
