# Ride-Sharing Readiness Checklist

Before launching a ride-sharing capability, confirm:

- Hold and commit are separate
- Freshness budgets exist
- Hard-stale evidence cannot commit
- Rematch budget exists
- Redispatch cap exists
- Pricing receipt exists
- Fairness guardrails are actionable
- Inference DAG has budgets
- Conflict handling is phase-aware
- Safety/fraud hard stops exist
- Replay card is emitted for high-risk decisions
