# ROCS: Ride-Sharing

| Dimension | Pressure | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Reliability | Match stability under movement. | Hold/commit separation, bounded rematch. |
| Observability | Explain match, price, reassignment. | Replay card and receipts. |
| Cost | Redispatch, recomputation, incentives. | DAG budgets and caps. |
| Sustainability | Compute and real-world route waste. | Churn reduction and efficient routing. |
