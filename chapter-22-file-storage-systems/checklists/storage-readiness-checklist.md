# Storage Readiness Checklist

Before launch, confirm:

- State claims have receipt gates
- `versionId` is immutable
- Stored and Discoverable are separate
- Metadata is authoritative for meaning/policy
- Indexes and AI derivatives are rebuildable
- Integrity proof includes hash, version, key lineage, verification, restore
- Lifecycle/delete are liability actions
- Legal hold and retention outrank cleanup
- Restore is demonstrated, not assumed
- Stop authority freezes unsafe transitions
