# Admissible Evidence Checklist

Before using evidence for action, check:

- Is the source authoritative?
- Is the data fresh enough?
- Is the version pinned?
- Is the user authorized to see it?
- Is sensitive data redacted?
- Does it conflict with another authoritative source?
- Is the evidence sufficient for the action?
- Can the evidence be replayed later?
- Does the evidence support commitment or only explanation?
