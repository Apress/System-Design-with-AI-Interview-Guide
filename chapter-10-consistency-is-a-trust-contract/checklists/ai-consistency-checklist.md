# AI Consistency Checklist

For AI responses, check:

- Is the evidence authoritative?
- Is the evidence fresh?
- Is the policy version pinned?
- Is the user authorized to see the evidence?
- Are sources conflicting?
- Is the assistant explaining or committing?
- Is the assistant using conversation history as evidence?
- Is the assistant relying on support articles for authoritative action?
- Should the assistant defer or escalate?
- Is replay evidence captured?
