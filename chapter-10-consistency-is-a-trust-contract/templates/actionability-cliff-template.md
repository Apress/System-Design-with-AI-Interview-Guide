## Journey Zones

| Zone | User Intent | Consistency Tolerance | Example State |
|---|---|---|---|
| Informational | Browsing or awareness | Higher tolerance | Estimated / approximate |
| Advisory | Guidance or recommendation | Some tolerance | Suggested / likely |
| Actionable | User can submit or commit | Low tolerance | Validated / eligible |
| Irreversible | Business is bound | Near-zero tolerance | Confirmed / settled / final |
