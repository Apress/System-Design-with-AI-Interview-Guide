# AI Consistency Review Prompt

Review my AI-enabled design for consistency risks.

Evaluate:
- Whether AI uses authoritative evidence
- Whether evidence is fresh and versioned
- Whether the user is authorized to see the evidence
- Whether sources conflict
- Whether the assistant is explaining or committing
- Whether inadmissible evidence is being used for action
- Whether defer/escalate/refuse behavior exists
- Whether the decision can be replayed

Then propose a safer admissible evidence gate.
