# Senior Consistency Defense Prompt

Challenge my consistency design as an interviewer.

Ask why eventual consistency is not enough and why availability alone is not the right goal at commitment points.

Evaluate whether I can defend:
- Consistency as a trust contract
- Actionability cliffs
- Strong vs eventual consistency by journey moment
- Semantic state labels
- Admissible evidence
- AI consistency boundaries
- Compensation paths
- Decision replay

Then give me a stronger senior-architect answer.
