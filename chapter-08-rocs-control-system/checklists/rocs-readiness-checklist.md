# ROCS Readiness Checklist

Before scaling an AI-enabled capability, confirm:

- Reliability promise is defined
- Commitment point is explicit
- Pending state exists
- Retry budget exists
- Idempotency exists
- Observability traces the full DAG
- Prompt-level tracing exists
- Vector telemetry exists
- GPU queue telemetry exists
- Decision replay exists
- Cost budgets exist
- Token budget exists
- Tool-call budget exists
- Retrieval fan-out budget exists
- Sustainability signals exist
- Stop authority exists
- Re-enable criteria exist
