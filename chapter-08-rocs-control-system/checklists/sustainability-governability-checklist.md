# Sustainability and Governability Checklist

Sustainability means responsible compute and governable change.

Check:

- Can policies change safely?
- Can models change safely?
- Can vendors change safely?
- Can regions change safely?
- Can team ownership change safely?
- Is carbon per inference measured?
- Are flexible jobs time-shifted?
- Is carbon-aware routing possible?
- Is quantization used where quality holds?
- Is unnecessary data movement reduced?
- Are useless hops eliminated?
