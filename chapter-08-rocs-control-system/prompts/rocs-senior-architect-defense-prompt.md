# ROCS Senior Architect Defense Prompt

Challenge my ROCS design as an interviewer.

Ask me why ROCS is necessary instead of simply monitoring latency, error rate, and cost.

Evaluate whether I can defend:

- Reliability beyond uptime
- Observability as decision replay
- Cost as behavior at scale
- Sustainability as governable change
- Inference as a DAG
- Budgets for retries, tools, retrieval, tokens, and GPU queues
- Stop authority and re-enable criteria

Then give me a stronger senior-architect answer.
