# Chapter 7 Exercises

## Exercise 1: LLM-SHIELD Warm-Up

Apply Scope, Harden, Isolate, Empower, Live-Defend, and Demonstrate to an AI assistant that helps customers modify orders, answer refund questions, summarize policies, and escalate cases.

## Exercise 2: Scope and Unacceptable Failures

Rewrite a vague AI-agent scope into concrete unacceptable failures, admissible evidence, forbidden actions, required citations, and human-review boundaries.

## Exercise 3: Prompt and Policy Hardening

Analyze a production assistant where prompts live in shared documents, refund policies live in a wiki, and guardrail thresholds are changed manually.

## Exercise 4: Retrieval and Tool Isolation

Design Zero Trust RAG for an order-modification assistant that retrieves from product pages, policy documents, support articles, CRM notes, order records, and warehouse updates.

## Exercise 5: Autonomy and Empowerment

Classify AI actions across autonomy levels: observe only, suggest, act with approval, bounded autonomy, and restricted.

## Exercise 6: Runtime Defense

Design the Live-Defend layer using AI firewalls, runtime policies, semantic anomaly detection, observability, SIEM/SOC integration, kill switches, and session replay.

## Exercise 7: Demonstrate and Prove Controls

Create the evidence package that proves the AI capability is still safe, compliant, monitored, and improving six months after launch.

## Exercise 8: Senior Architect LLM-SHIELD Design Review

Use LLM-SHIELD to review and improve an AI-enabled order modification capability.
