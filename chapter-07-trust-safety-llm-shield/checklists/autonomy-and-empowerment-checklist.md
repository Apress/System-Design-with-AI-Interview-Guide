# Autonomy and Empowerment Checklist

Before granting autonomy, ask:

- What is the autonomy level?
- Is the action reversible?
- Is the risk low enough?
- Is evidence complete?
- Is confidence above threshold?
- Is approval required?
- Is there a rate limit?
- Is there a rollback path?
- Is there a kill switch?
- Is the action replayable?
- Can autonomy be narrowed by tenant, region, cohort, or tool path?
