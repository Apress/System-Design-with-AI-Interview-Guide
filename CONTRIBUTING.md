# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in improving this companion repository. Contributions should support the book’s learning experience without reproducing the book.

## Good Contributions

Good contributions include:

- Corrections to typos or broken links.
- Improvements to templates or prompts.
- Additional practice questions.
- Clearer glossary definitions.
- Diagram improvements that clarify responsibility or flow.
- Small examples that help readers practice.

## Contributions to Avoid

Please do not submit:

- Full chapter text.
- Large excerpts from the book.
- Complete replacement explanations for book chapters.
- Vendor marketing content.
- Production claims that have not been validated.
- Sensitive, proprietary, or customer-confidential architecture details.

## Style Guidelines

- Keep content concise and practical.
- Point readers to the book for full explanations.
- Prefer templates, checklists, prompts, and exercises over long essays.
- Use clear Markdown.
- Define acronyms on first use where helpful.
- Keep examples educational and vendor-neutral unless a chapter intentionally discusses a vendor.

## Pull Request Checklist

Before submitting a change, confirm:

- The change supports the companion-lab purpose.
- It does not reproduce copyrighted book content.
- It does not present educational examples as production advice.
- It improves clarity, practice value, or navigation.
