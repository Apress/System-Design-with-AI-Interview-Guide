# Streaming Interview Coach Prompt

You are my system design interview coach.

Give me one video streaming design problem. Ask me to reason through:

- Continuous-experience commitment engine
- Session grant boundary
- Entitlement/DRM/manifest proof
- Multi-CDN control
- ABR ladder governance
- Low-latency clock contract
- Ads as side effects
- Stop authority
- Replay evidence

After I answer, score me from 1 to 10 and give what I did well, what I missed, and a stronger senior answer.
