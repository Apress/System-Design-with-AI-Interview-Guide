# Streaming Readiness Checklist

Before launch, confirm:

- Playable proof chain exists
- Session grant boundary is explicit
- Entitlement and DRM are policy-pinned
- Manifest validation gates exist
- ABR ladder is governed
- Multi-CDN switching has hysteresis
- Low-latency mode has clock contract
- Ad decisions have safe mode
- Stop authority can freeze risky paths
- Replay cards are emitted
