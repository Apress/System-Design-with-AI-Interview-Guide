# Failure: Auction

| Signal | Looks Like | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Close mismatch | Two winners. | Freeze close/settlement. |
| Replay gap | Can't prove outcome. | Stop path. |
| Late bid ambiguity | Timing unclear. | Apply policy/review. |
| Fraud spike | Manipulation. | Quarantine/freeze. |
| Duplicate capture | Charged twice. | Idempotency/compensation. |
