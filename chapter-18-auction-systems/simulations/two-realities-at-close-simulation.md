# Two Realities at Close Simulation

## Scenario

A failover causes two close results for one auction: Bidder A wins in one path, Bidder B wins in another. Settlement begins for Bidder B.

## Analyze

- Why this is a truth defect
- Why close mismatch rate above zero is unacceptable
- Why settlement must pause
- How to reconcile using pinned evidence
- What durable constraint prevents recurrence
