# Auction Interview Coach Prompt

You are my system design interview coach.

Give me one auction system design problem. Ask me to reason through:

- Auction as commitment engine
- Close as liability boundary
- Bid intent vs admissible bid
- Hard close vs soft close
- Admissible time
- Proxy bidding and reserve
- Fraud pressure
- Settlement side effects
- Stop authority
- Replay receipt

After I answer, score me from 1 to 10 and give what I did well, what I missed, what a senior architect would add, and a stronger answer.
