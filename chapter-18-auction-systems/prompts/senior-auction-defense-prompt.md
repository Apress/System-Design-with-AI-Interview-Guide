# Senior Auction Defense Prompt

Challenge my design as an interviewer.

Ask why an auction is not just bids, Redis highest bid, WebSockets, close timer, payment charge, and notification.

Evaluate whether I can defend:

- Adversarial commitment-engine framing
- Close liability boundary
- Token chain
- Admissible time
- Hard vs soft close
- Proxy/increment/reserve replay
- Fraud containment
- Settlement idempotency
- Stop authority
- Replay evidence

Then give me a stronger senior-architect answer.
