# AI Failure Checklist

Check whether AI can:

- Act on stale evidence
- Act on non-authoritative evidence
- Continue when sources conflict
- Call tools without authority
- Produce a commitment without proof
- Bypass human review
- Hide uncertainty behind fluent language

Controls:

- Admissible evidence gate
- Authority envelope
- Confidence threshold
- Tool budget
- Human approval
- Defer/escalate behavior
- Replay card
