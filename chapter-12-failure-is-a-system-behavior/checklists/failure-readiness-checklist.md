# Failure Readiness Checklist

Before releasing a high-risk capability, confirm:

- Uncertainty entry points are mapped
- Commitment points are identified
- Blast radius is intentionally scoped
- Retry budgets exist
- Idempotency exists
- Pending state exists
- Escalation contract exists
- Hard-stop rules exist
- AI evidence gates exist
- Recovery behavior is defined
- Replay evidence is captured
