# Failure Interview Coach Prompt

You are my system design interview coach.

Give me one failure-sensitive system design problem. Ask me to reason through:

- Where uncertainty enters
- How failure propagates
- Which commitment point is at risk
- Blast radius
- Retry amplification
- Degradation states
- Escalation contract
- AI confident wrongness, if relevant
- Hard stop conditions
- Replay evidence

After I answer, score me from 1 to 10 and give:
1. What I did well
2. What I missed
3. What a senior architect would add
4. A stronger version of my answer
