# Senior Failure Defense Prompt

Challenge my failure-handling design as an interviewer.

Ask why retries, circuit breakers, fallbacks, and alerts are not enough.

Evaluate whether I can defend:

- Failure as behavior, not event
- Uncertainty path
- Blast-radius design
- Safe vs dangerous retries
- Pending/degraded/escalated/hard-stop states
- AI confident wrongness
- Escalation as contract
- Replay evidence

Then give me a stronger senior-architect answer.
