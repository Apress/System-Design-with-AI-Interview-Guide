# Chapter 2 Companion Resources: Understanding the Business Domain

This folder contains companion practice material for Chapter 2 of *System Design with AI Interview Guide*.

Use these resources after reading the chapter to practice domain discovery, actor and journey analysis, capability mapping, and AI opportunity boundary definition.

For the full explanation, narrative examples, and chapter walkthrough, refer to the book.

## What is included

- `exercises.md` — the Chapter 2 practice questions.
- `solution-guide.md` — guided sample answers and evaluation notes.
- `templates/` — reusable worksheets for domain discovery and capability mapping.
- `checklists/` — question banks and readiness checks.
- `prompts/` — AI-coach prompts for practice and review.
- `diagrams/` — Mermaid diagrams that visualize domain-to-architecture thinking.

## Recommended use

1. Read Chapter 2 in the book.
2. Answer the questions in `exercises.md` without looking at the solution guide.
3. Fill out the templates for a system design problem of your choice.
4. Compare your response with `solution-guide.md`.
5. Use the prompts to rehearse and improve your answer.
