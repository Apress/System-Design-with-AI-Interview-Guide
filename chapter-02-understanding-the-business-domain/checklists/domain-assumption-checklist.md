# Domain Assumption Checklist

Use this checklist to avoid building the wrong system confidently.

## User Assumptions

- [ ] I know the primary actor.
- [ ] I know the secondary actors.
- [ ] I understand what each actor is trying to accomplish.
- [ ] I understand which journey matters most.

## Business Assumptions

- [ ] I know the business objective.
- [ ] I know the most important success metric.
- [ ] I know which trade-off the business would accept.
- [ ] I know which trade-off the business would reject.

## Domain Entity Assumptions

- [ ] I identified the core entities.
- [ ] I know which entities represent commitment or money.
- [ ] I know which service or capability should own each source of truth.
- [ ] I identified lifecycle states for critical entities.

## Architecture Risk Assumptions

- [ ] I know which flow must be correct over merely fast.
- [ ] I know which flow must be highly available.
- [ ] I know which flow must be auditable.
- [ ] I know where duplicate actions can happen.
- [ ] I know where stale data can harm users.

## AI Assumptions

- [ ] I know which AI use cases create business value.
- [ ] I know which AI use cases are risky or over-scoped.
- [ ] I defined what AI may suggest, decide, or act on.
- [ ] I defined fallback behavior for low confidence.
- [ ] I defined evidence and replay requirements.
