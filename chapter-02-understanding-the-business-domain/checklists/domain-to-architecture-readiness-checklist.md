# Domain-to-Architecture Readiness Checklist

Before drawing the architecture, confirm that the domain is design-ready.

## Ready to Draw When You Can State

- [ ] The core business goal in one sentence.
- [ ] The primary journey in one sentence.
- [ ] The top three capabilities.
- [ ] The core entities and their owners.
- [ ] The trust moment where the system must not lie.
- [ ] The quality attributes that matter most.
- [ ] The highest-risk failure mode.
- [ ] The AI authority boundary, if AI is involved.
- [ ] The metric that would prove the design works.

## Not Ready If You Are Still Guessing

- [ ] Who the user is.
- [ ] What action creates commitment.
- [ ] Where money, inventory, identity, or compliance enters the design.
- [ ] Whether correctness or latency matters more.
- [ ] Whether AI can act or only recommend.
- [ ] Who owns the source of truth.
