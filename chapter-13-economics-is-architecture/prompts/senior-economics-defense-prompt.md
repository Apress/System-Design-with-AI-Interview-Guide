# Senior Economics Defense Prompt

Challenge my economics design as an interviewer.

Ask why cost optimization is not simply monitoring spend, using smaller models, trimming prompts, and buying reserved capacity.

Evaluate whether I can defend:

- Economics as architecture under volume
- Unit of value
- Unit cost
- Cost multipliers
- FinOps as runtime enforcement
- Stop authority
- Sustainability as execution behavior
- Evidence of value produced at acceptable cost

Then give me a stronger senior-architect answer.
