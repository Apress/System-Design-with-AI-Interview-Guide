# Capability Discovery Checklist

Before designing services, clarify:

- What must the business be able to do?
- Which outcomes matter most?
- Who are the primary actors?
- What are the core business entities?
- Which capabilities are customer-facing?
- Which capabilities are operational?
- Which capabilities are financial?
- Which capabilities are risk-related?
- Which capabilities are intelligence-driven?
- Which capabilities differentiate the business?
- Which capabilities are supporting or commodity?
