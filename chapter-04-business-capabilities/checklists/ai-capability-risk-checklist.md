# AI Capability Risk Checklist

Before placing AI inside a capability, ask:

- Is the output informational, advisory, or committing?
- Can incorrect output cause financial, legal, safety, or trust damage?
- Does AI need real-time state?
- Is the required data fresh, complete, and authorized?
- Should AI be inside the critical path?
- Does the AI output require deterministic validation?
- Is human review required?
- What fallback exists when confidence is low?
- What evidence must be cited?
- Can the decision be replayed later?
