# Ownership Clarity Checklist

For each important customer promise, define:

- Who owns the promise?
- Who owns the system of record?
- Who can commit the decision?
- Who can reverse the decision?
- Who owns customer-visible messaging?
- Who owns recovery?
- Who owns audit evidence?
- Which event proves the commitment happened?
- Which correlation ID connects the journey?
- Which team is paged when the promise fails?
