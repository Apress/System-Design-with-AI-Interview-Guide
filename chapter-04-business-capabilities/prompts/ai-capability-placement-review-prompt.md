# AI Capability Placement Review Prompt

Review my AI capability placement.

For each AI feature, classify it as:

- Embedded inside a business capability
- Shared intelligence service
- Human-reviewed decision support
- Outside the critical path

Evaluate:

- Business value
- Data needs
- Latency requirement
- Risk of incorrect output
- Authority to act
- Evidence required
- Fallback behavior
- Replay needs

Then recommend a safer and more scalable placement.
