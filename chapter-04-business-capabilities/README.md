# Chapter 4 Companion Resources: Business Capabilities

This folder contains companion practice material for Chapter 4 of *System Design with AI Interview Guide*.

Use these resources after reading the chapter to practice business capability mapping, capability boundary design, ownership analysis, AI capability placement, and capability-to-architecture translation.

For the full explanation, narrative examples, and chapter walkthrough, refer to the book.

## Contents

- `exercises.md` — end-of-chapter practice questions and a bonus exercise.
- `solution-guide.md` — expanded solution guidance, senior architect additions, and common mistakes.
- `templates/` — reusable templates for capability mapping and boundary design.
- `checklists/` — practical checklists for capability discovery, ownership clarity, and AI risk.
- `prompts/` — AI coaching prompts for practice and self-review.
- `diagrams/` — Mermaid diagrams for capability maps and AI placement decisions.
