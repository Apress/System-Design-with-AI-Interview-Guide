# Customer Journey Discovery Checklist

Before designing services, clarify:

- Who is the primary customer?
- What is the customer trying to accomplish?
- What is the happy path?
- What are the high-friction steps?
- Where does the customer need the system to be correct?
- Where can approximation be acceptable?
- Which steps require confirmation?
- Which failures should be visible to the user?
- Which failures should be hidden through recovery?
- What evidence is needed for support?
