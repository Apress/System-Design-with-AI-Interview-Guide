# Trust Moment Checklist

A trust moment exists when the user believes the system has made a promise.

Check for:

- Payment accepted
- Order confirmed
- Inventory reserved
- Delivery date promised
- Refund approved
- Account updated
- Password/security change confirmed
- AI assistant gives policy, pricing, or order-status answer

For each trust moment, define:

- The promise
- The system of record
- The authority to commit
- Failure behavior
- Evidence needed
- Replay path
