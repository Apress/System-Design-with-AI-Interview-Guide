# AI Assistant Empathy Review Prompt

Review my AI customer assistant design.

Evaluate:

- Whether the assistant improves the customer journey
- Where it may overpromise
- Where deterministic validation is required
- Where evidence or citation is needed
- What should happen when confidence is low
- What should be escalated to a human
- What should be logged for replay

Give me a safer and more customer-trustworthy version of the design.
