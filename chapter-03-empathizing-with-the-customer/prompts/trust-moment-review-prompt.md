# Trust Moment Review Prompt

Review my system design answer and identify the trust moments.

For each trust moment, tell me:

- What promise the user believes was made
- What backend truth must exist
- Which service owns the commitment
- What can fail
- Whether the system should retry, degrade, refuse, or escalate
- What evidence should be captured
- Whether AI is allowed to explain, suggest, or commit anything related to it

Then rewrite my answer to make the trust moments clearer.
