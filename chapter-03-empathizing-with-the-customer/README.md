# Chapter 3 Companion Resources: Empathizing with the Customer

This folder contains companion practice material for Chapter 3 of *System Design with AI Interview Guide*.

Use these resources after reading the chapter to practice customer journey mapping, empathy analysis, trust-moment identification, failure-moment analysis, and AI assistant boundary design.

For the full explanation, narrative examples, and chapter walkthrough, refer to the book.

## Contents

- `exercises.md` — End-of-chapter practice questions and bonus exercise.
- `solution-guide.md` — Guided sample answers, senior-architect additions, and common mistakes.
- `templates/` — Reusable templates for customer journeys, empathy maps, trust moments, failure moments, and AI assistant boundaries.
- `checklists/` — Interview-ready checklists for customer journey discovery, trust moments, friction, and experience-to-architecture readiness.
- `prompts/` — AI-coach prompts for practicing and reviewing customer-empathy-driven system design answers.
- `diagrams/` — Mermaid diagrams that connect customer experience to architecture.
