# ROCS: Social Networking

| Dimension | Pressure | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Reliability | Bounded influence under viral spread. | Promotion caps, freeze paths. |
| Observability | Reach decisions need receipts. | Replay cards and policy pins. |
| Cost | Reranking, rechecking, notifying, moderation rework. | DAG budgets and fanout caps. |
| Sustainability | Trust survives repeated policy pressure. | Fairness/safety guardrails and stop authority. |
