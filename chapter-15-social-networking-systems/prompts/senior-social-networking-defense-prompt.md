# Senior Social Networking Defense Prompt

Challenge my social-networking design as an interviewer.

Ask why this is not just users, posts, followers, feed ranking, Kafka, Redis, and graph database.

Evaluate whether I can defend:

- Governed influence engine framing
- Visibility vs amplification
- Recommendation mode boundary
- Admissible evidence
- Surface authority
- HVI stop authority
- Distribution DAG budgets
- Replay evidence

Then give me a stronger senior-architect answer.
