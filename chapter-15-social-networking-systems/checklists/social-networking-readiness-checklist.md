# Social Networking Readiness Checklist

Before launching a social influence capability, confirm:

- Visibility and amplification are separated
- Surface authority matrix exists
- Recommendation mode changes data path
- Admissible evidence gate exists
- Engagement cannot create admissibility
- Distribution DAG budgets exist
- HVI stop authority is runnable
- Replay coverage hard stop exists
- Moderation and appeal receipts exist
- Fairness and safety guardrails are monitored
