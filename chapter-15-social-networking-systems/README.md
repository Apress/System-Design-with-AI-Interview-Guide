# Chapter 15 Companion Resources: Social Networking Systems

This folder contains companion practice material for Chapter 15 of *System Design with AI Interview Guide*.

Use these resources after reading the chapter to practice designing social networking systems as governed influence engines. The focus is not only users, posts, likes, followers, and feeds. The focus is visibility versus amplification, recommendation-mode boundaries, surface authority, admissible evidence, Harm Velocity Index stop authority, distribution-DAG budgets, fairness and safety guardrails, and replayable proof.

## How to use this folder

1. Start with `exercises.md`.
2. Compare your answers with `solution-guide.md`.
3. Use schemas and policies to make the design concrete.
4. Use worksheets to apply the earlier lenses from the book.
5. Use simulations and test cases to pressure-test the design.
6. Use prompts to practice senior-level interview defense.

For the full explanation, narrative examples, and chapter walkthrough, refer to the book.
