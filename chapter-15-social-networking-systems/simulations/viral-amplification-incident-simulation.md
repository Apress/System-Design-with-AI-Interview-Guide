# Simulation: Viral Amplification Incident

## Scenario

A post is granted follower visibility and then starts qualifying for cross-surface discovery. Engagement is high, but Tier 2 provenance confidence is low.

## Tasks

- Decide whether to mint amplificationToken.
- Apply admissible-evidence gates.
- Compute HVI from severity, velocity, and uncertainty.
- Decide throttle, freeze, review, or suppress.
- Produce replay fields.
