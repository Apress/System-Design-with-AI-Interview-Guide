# 30-Day Practice Plan

## Week 1: Liability Boundary Map
Deliver a one-page map showing where the system stops attempting and starts owing.

## Week 2: Admissibility and Freshness Contract
Define what evidence is valid enough to act and what happens when stale, missing, or conflicting.

## Week 3: Churn Budget and Stop Authority
Cap retries, fan-out, time, tool calls, tokens, and cost. Define Normal, Conservative, Defer, Freeze, and Human Review modes.

## Week 4: Decision Replay Cards
Emit replay-ready proof for one critical workflow and test if another team can reconstruct the decision.
