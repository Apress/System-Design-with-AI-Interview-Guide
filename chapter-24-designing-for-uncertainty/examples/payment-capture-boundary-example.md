# Payment Capture Boundary Example

## Promise

The platform makes a specific user-visible or business-visible claim.

## Commitment Boundary

Define the exact moment where attempting becomes owing.

## Required Evidence

- Source-of-truth state
- Freshness window
- Policy version
- Authority
- Budget status
- Receipt path

## Stop Authority

If evidence weakens, defer, freeze, suppress, route to review, or return pending rather than committing falsely.
