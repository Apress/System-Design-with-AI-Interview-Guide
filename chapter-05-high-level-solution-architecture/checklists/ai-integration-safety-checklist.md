# AI Integration Safety Checklist

Before adding AI to the architecture, ask:

- What capability does AI support?
- Is AI advisory, assistive, or automated?
- Is AI in the critical path?
- What evidence does AI use?
- Is the evidence authorized?
- Is the evidence fresh enough?
- Can AI call tools?
- What tools are forbidden?
- What deterministic validation is required?
- What happens when confidence is low?
- What should AI refuse to answer?
- What should be logged for replay?
- Who owns the AI-assisted outcome?
