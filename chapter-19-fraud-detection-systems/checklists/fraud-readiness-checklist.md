# Fraud Readiness Checklist

Before launching fraud enforcement, confirm:

- riskDecisionId is advisory only
- stepUpChallengeId exists for reversible proof
- enforcementId marks liability boundary
- liability tokens are separate by action
- evidence-tier policy exists
- freshness and label-delay policy exists
- reversible control ladder exists
- appeals and reversals are linked
- stop authority can freeze enforcement
- replay coverage hard stop exists
