# Appeal Overturn Spike Simulation

## Scenario

A new refund-denial threshold ships. Refund denials increase 35%. Appeals overturn 28% of denials in one cohort.

## Expected Controls

- Freeze refund denial for affected cohort
- Shift to step-up and case review
- Preserve replay cards
- Roll back or quarantine policy version
- Re-enable only after overturn rate returns within bounds
