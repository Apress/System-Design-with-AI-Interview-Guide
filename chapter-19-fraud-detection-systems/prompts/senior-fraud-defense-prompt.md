Challenge my design as an interviewer.

Ask why fraud is not simply events, feature store, ML model, score, rules, case queue, and blocking.

Evaluate whether I can defend:

- Authority allocation framing
- Advisory vs enforcement tokens
- Evidence tiers
- Label delay
- Reversibility
- Stop authority
- ROCS
- Replay proof

Then give me a stronger senior-architect answer.
