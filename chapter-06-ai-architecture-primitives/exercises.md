# Chapter 6 Exercises

## Exercise 1: Six-Primitives Warm-Up

Apply Authority, Commitment, Time, Degradation, Evidence/Replay, and Ownership to an AI assistant that can help customers modify orders, answer refund questions, recommend products, and escalate support issues.

## Exercise 2: Authority Boundary

Analyze a customer-support AI agent that has broad permissions to issue refunds, modify orders, send emails, and close tickets. Define the correct authority envelope.

## Exercise 3: Commitment Point

Analyze a failure where an AI assistant tells a customer, “Your order has been modified,” before ERP and warehouse confirmation are complete.

## Exercise 4: Time Policy

Decide which clock governs an order-modification cutoff when event time, ingestion time, and decision time disagree.

## Exercise 5: Degradation and Refusal Lines

Design degradation behavior for a refund AI agent when policy retrieval is stale, customer history is incomplete, and payment systems are slow.

## Exercise 6: Decision Replay Card

Design the minimum evidence package required to answer, “Why did the system allow this refund?”

## Exercise 7: Ownership and Stop Authority

Design a Stop Authority SOP for AI-assisted order modifications when disputes, ERP failures, and replay gaps increase.

## Exercise 8: Senior Architect Design Review

Use the six primitives to review and improve an AI-enabled order modification capability.
