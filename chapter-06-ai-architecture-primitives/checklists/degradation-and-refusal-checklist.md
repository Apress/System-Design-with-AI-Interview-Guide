# Degradation And Refusal Checklist

- Continue normally
- Use cached or safe-default behavior
- Move from automated to assistive
- Move to pending review
- Escalate to a human
- Refuse the action
- Hard stop the capability
- Missing evidence
- Stale policy
- Low confidence
- Tool failures
- External dependency delay
- High-risk customer state
- Replay coverage gap
- Increased disputes
