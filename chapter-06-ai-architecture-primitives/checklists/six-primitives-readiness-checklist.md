# Six Primitives Readiness Checklist

- Authority is bounded by represented role
- Acting identity is clear
- Tool allowlist exists
- Data scope is defined
- Commitment points are explicit
- Provisional and committed states are separate
- Governing clock is declared
- Degradation ladder exists
- Refusal lines are written
- Decision Replay Card exists
- Policy and guardrail versions are recorded
- Capability owner is named
- Stop authority is real
- Re-enable criteria are defined
