# Ownership Stop Authority Checklist

- Named capability owner
- Backup owner
- Monitored signals
- Numeric thresholds
- Stop/degrade/defer/throttle actions
- Blast-radius controls
- Customer/support communication plan
- Re-enable criteria
- Post-incident review owner
- No one can pause automation means ownership is incomplete
- Vague thresholds mean ownership is incomplete
- Undefined re-enable criteria mean ownership is incomplete
