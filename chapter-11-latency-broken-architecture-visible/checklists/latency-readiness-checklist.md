# Latency Readiness Checklist

Before launching a latency-sensitive capability, confirm:

- Journey moments are mapped
- Commitment points are identified
- Latency budgets exist by journey moment
- Tail-latency behavior is considered
- User retry behavior is controlled
- Idempotency exists
- Retry budgets exist
- Backpressure exists
- Priority queues are defined
- Safe degraded states exist
- Pending/failed/confirmed states are explicit
- AI DAG budgets exist, if AI is involved
- Replay evidence is captured
