# Senior Latency Defense Prompt

Challenge my latency design as an interviewer.

Ask why CDN, caching, async processing, and autoscaling are not enough.

Evaluate whether I can defend:

- Journey-weighted latency
- Commitment points
- Tail-latency behavior loops
- Idempotency and retry budgets
- Backpressure and priority queues
- Honest pending/failed/confirmed states
- AI DAG latency budgets
- Replay evidence

Then give me a stronger senior-architect answer.
